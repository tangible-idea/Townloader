Save posts, find their places, and read long threads, all in one app.

Townloader turns public Instagram and Threads posts into something you can use later. Save a post about a trip or a restaurant and its places land on your map. Read a long thread from start to finish. Keep the photos and videos you want.

■ Find the places in a post
Paste public Instagram post links into the Links tab. Townloader reads the caption and the text in the post's photos, finds the places mentioned, and gives each post a short title.

■ See them on a map
Open a category or a single post on the map. Drag posts between categories, remove places you don't need, and undo if you change your mind.

■ Organize with categories
Create categories like "Tokyo trip". New posts are sorted into a matching category automatically, and you can move them at any time.

■ Share a map with a link (optional)
Sign in and share a category or post map as a link. Friends can open it in the app or a browser. Sharing makes that map public to anyone with the link, and you can stop sharing at any time.

■ Whole threads, start to finish
Threads posts split into many parts (1/17, 2/17 ...) are laid out on one screen in the order the author wrote them, with the public replies.

■ AI summaries (optional)
After you agree in the app, get a short summary of a thread or its replies, and rate it with a thumbs up or down. You can also have replies read aloud.

■ Watch right there
Tap a video to play it in place. Long-press a post to preview its photos and videos.

■ Keep what you want in original quality
Select the parts you want or save everything at once, at the highest quality or the smallest size. Files go to the "Townloader" album in Photos, or to the Files app.

■ Open from the share button
Tap Share → Townloader in the app you're using, and the post opens in Townloader. Nothing is saved until you choose.

■ Browse public accounts
See a public Instagram account's posts, reels and stories, and save only the ones you want.

■ Account is optional
You can use Townloader without an account. Sign in with Apple or Google to back up your saved links and places across devices and share maps. You can delete your account in Settings at any time.

Also includes dark mode, and Korean and English.

Supported links
• Public Threads posts and threads
• Instagram posts, reels and multi-photo posts
• Instagram stories and highlights

Please note
• Only content from public accounts can be viewed. Private accounts are not supported.
