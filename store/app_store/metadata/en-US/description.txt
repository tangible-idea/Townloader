Long threads, photos and videos, all on one screen.

Townloader lets you read public Threads and Instagram posts in a clean view and keep only the ones you want. Paste a link, or pick Townloader from the share button in the app you're browsing.

■ Whole threads, start to finish
Threads posts split into many parts (1/17, 2/17 ...) are laid out on one screen in the order the author wrote them. You can see the public replies too.

■ Watch right there
Tap a video to play it in place. Long-press a post to preview its photos and videos full size.

■ Keep only what you want
Select the parts of a thread you want to keep and save their photos and videos, or save everything at once.

■ Original quality
Photos are saved at the largest resolution and videos at the highest quality. To save space, choose "Smallest size" in Settings.

■ Open from the share button
Tap Share → Townloader in the app you're using, and the post opens in Townloader. You choose what to keep. Tap "Open original" to go back to the original post at any time.

■ Browse public accounts
See a public Instagram account's posts, reels and stories as a list, and save only the ones you want.

■ Neatly organized in Photos or Files
Saved files go to the "Townloader" album in Photos, or to the Files app if you choose so in Settings. Share a file or open where it was saved straight from the Downloads list.

Supported links
• Public Threads posts and threads
• Instagram posts, reels and multi-photo posts
• Instagram stories and highlights

• Only content from public accounts can be viewed. Private accounts are not supported.
