2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Townloader lets people save photos and videos from public Instagram and Threads posts to their own device in the original quality, for personal offline viewing and backup. A typical user is a creator backing up their own posts, or someone keeping a public post they want to look at again later.
Problem it solves: those apps do not offer a way to save the original media file. Townloader takes a post link and saves the media to the Photos app in one step.
Audience: general adult users of Instagram and Threads.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP
No account, login or setup is needed.
1. Open the app. The Download tab is shown.
2. Paste a public post link and tap the arrow button. Sample links:
   - Instagram: [INSTAGRAM_SAMPLE_LINK]
   - Threads: [THREADS_SAMPLE_LINK]
3. The post appears with a preview. Tap "Download all". Allow photo access when asked.
4. The Downloads tab shows progress. Finished files are saved to the "Townloader" album in Photos. Each item has a Share button and a folder button that opens Photos (or Files, if album saving is turned off).
5. Share extension: in the Instagram or Threads app, tap Share on a post and choose Townloader (under "More" the first time). The app opens and starts the download.
6. Profile tab: enter a public username (for example "instagram") and tap Open to browse that account's posts, reels and stories.
7. Settings: choose download quality and whether to save to Photos.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- HikerAPI (hikerapi.com): a third-party data service used to look up public Instagram posts and get their media links. The app sends only the post code or username from the link the user entered. The API key is built into the app.
- Threads public embed pages (threads.com): the public, logged-out embed page of a Threads post, read to find its media.
- Instagram and Threads content delivery networks: the photo and video files are downloaded directly from there.
The app uses no authentication, payment, analytics, advertising or AI services, and it collects no personal data.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app has the same features and content in all regions. The interface is in English, or Korean on devices set to Korean. Lookups need Instagram and Threads to be reachable, so they fail in regions where those services are blocked.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry. It does not host, include or redistribute any third-party content. At the user's request, it retrieves media that is already public without logging in and saves it to that user's own device. It cannot access private accounts. The App Store description tells users that downloaded content belongs to its creators, that it is for personal use only, and that Townloader is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram, Threads or Meta.
